Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Arkive is an animal health record keeping app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information we collect

When you use Arkive, we collect the following:

  • Account information — your email address and optionally your phone number, used for account authentication and recovery.
  • Animal health records — names, species, vaccination records, medications, vet visit notes, weight entries and custom fields that you enter manually.
  • Device preferences — notification settings and sign-in preferences stored locally on your device.

How we use your information

  • To provide cloud backup and sync your animal health records across devices.
  • To send push notifications for vaccination reminders, medication schedules and birthdays.
  • To allow account recovery if you lose access to your email.

Data storage

Your data is stored in two places:

  • Locally on your device — using SQLite for offline access.
  • In the cloud — using Supabase, a secure cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Supabase is hosted on AWS infrastructure.

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade or share your personal data with third parties. Your animal health records are private to your account only. No other users can access your data.

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — cloud database and authentication (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Resend — transactional email delivery for confirmation and password reset emails (resend.com/privacy)
  • Google Play — app distribution and in-app purchases (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (revenuecat.com/privacy)

Notifications

Arkive sends local push notifications for vaccination reminders, medication schedules, birthdays and custom field reminders. You can disable notifications at any time in the app's Account settings or in your device's notification settings.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

You can delete individual animals and all their records at any time from within the app.

Children's privacy

Arkive is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data associated with your account
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data via PDF reports
  • Correct inaccurate data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@arkiveapp.it.com or visit our account deletion page.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), encrypted data storage, and secure authentication tokens. However no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or an in-app notification. Continued use of Arkive after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at:
info@arkiveapp.it.com
